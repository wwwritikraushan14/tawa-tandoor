Namaste, and welcome

One kitchen, two traditions

Half our menu comes off the tawa — dosas, paneer and slow-cooked dals. The other half comes out of the tandoor — tikka, kebabs and butter chicken. Veg and non-veg, cooked in the same kitchen, with the same care.
